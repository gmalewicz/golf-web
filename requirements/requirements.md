# Requirements

| ID    | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| CR-01 | Display tee for each round that is part of tournament and is displayed on tournament results screen after displaying individual rounds. The tee name shall be displayed after Strokes Net. |
| CR-02 | Add possibility to change tee on edit scorecard screen (addScorecard). |
| CR-03 | Allow the tournament owner to edit a round that has been assigned to their tournament, without any backend changes. When the round is saved, it shall automatically be removed from the tournament and re-added. Editing by the tournament owner is permitted only while the tournament is not closed. The round owner shall not be able to edit a round that belongs to a tournament. |
| CR-04 | Update historical round results for `round-view-fb-mp`. Players shall be assigned to a team based on the `team` field (optional, but mandatory for that format) in `PlayerRoundDetails`. Currently players are assigned to teams arbitrarily. |
| CR-05 | In the cycle results table, add two additional columns after the player's position indicator. The first column shall display an up arrow (▲) if the player has improved their position after adding the latest tournament, a down arrow (▼) if the position has worsened, or a circle (●) if the position is unchanged. The second column shall display the number of positions gained (positive) or lost (negative), or remain empty if the position has not changed. The backend shall return the player's previous position in a new field `oldPlace` within the `CycleResult` interface, for each series. |
| CR-06 | Implement the Four Ball Stroke Play (`Format.FOUR_BALL_STROKE_PLAY`) format for the online round definition and the online round score card, in the same way as Four Ball Match Play. **Online round definition (`online-round-def`, `player-selector`):** enable the currently disabled "Four Ball SP" tab. Keep the existing 3 or 4 player selection (1 and 2 players disabled). With 4 players, players 1-2 are team 1 and players 3-4 are team 2. With 3 players, all players are team 1. Show the team number next to each player, as for Four Ball MP. Show a handicap allowance radio selection (100%, 90%, 85%, default 85%) and store the selected value in the `mpFormat` field of each `OnlineRound`. Set the `team` field of each `OnlineRound` to the player's team. All players shall use tees of the same type (number of holes); otherwise the round is not started. After the round is created, navigate to `/scorecard/onlineFbStrokeplay`. **Online round score card (`online-fb-strokeplay`):** replace the current empty `OnlineFbStrokeplayComponent` with a full score card component that extends `OnlineRoundBaseComponent` (like `OnlineFbMatchplayComponent`). It shall use `app-common-scorecard-top` to enter strokes, putts and penalties for the round owner, and show a Refresh button for other users. Each player's playing handicap shall be the unrounded course HCP multiplied by the allowance (`mpFormat`) and rounded. It is not reduced relative to the lowest handicap. Handicap strokes per hole shall be highlighted as in Four Ball MP. The score card table shall show the players grouped under team headers (Team 1 / Team 2), the hole number, each player's gross strokes with putts and penalties. The gross stroke of the player whose net score counts for the team on a hole (the team's best net score, all tied players marked) shall be displayed in red. The total net result of each team is displayed in brackets next to the team header, e.g. "Team 1 (72)"; there are no per-team net columns and no total row. The team net score for a hole is the lower net score (strokes minus hole HCP strokes) of the team's players, counted in the team total only after all players of that team have entered a score for the hole. A picked-up ball (`x`) shall not count as the team's score unless all team players picked up, in which case the hole is excluded from the total. Delete, Finalize and Info buttons shall work as in Four Ball MP. `my-online-score-card` and `info` already navigate to `/scorecard/onlineFbStrokeplay`, so continuing an unfinished round shall open the new score card. **Online preview (`view-selector`, `online-score-card-view`):** add a new "FB-SP Rounds" tab listing Four Ball Stroke Play rounds (players grouped by team, course, tee time, status) and a new `ViewType.FBSP` opening the online score card view. The view shall show, grouped by team, each player's gross strokes per hole (the counting score in red, as on the online score card) and the handicap strokes per hole highlighted with the same colours as on the online score card and, for each team, the net result per hole with OUT, IN and TOTAL columns, using the same handicap allowance rules as the online score card. The view shall be updated live when new scores are received. **Historical round view (`round`):** for rounds with `Format.FOUR_BALL_STROKE_PLAY` add a "Four Ball Stroke Play" tab (new `round-view-fb-sp` component) presenting the same results as the online preview: per team net result per hole with OUT, IN and TOTAL, and per player gross strokes with handicap strokes highlighted and counting scores in red. The winning team result on each hole shall be marked with a red circle as in match play (no mark on ties); the totals are not marked. Players are assigned to teams using the `team` field of `PlayerRoundDetails`. **Out of scope:** tournament support. |
